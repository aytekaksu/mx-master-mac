The unmodified upstream vector is preserved as `apple-magic-trackpad.svg`.

For the MX Master Mac logo, the two device paths are retained, converted to black-and-white shapes, given heavier outlines, and scaled. Gradients and editor metadata are omitted from the composition. A separate mouse illustration is placed on the trackpad surface. The dark variant lightens the trackpad outline.
